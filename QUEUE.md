# WinVamp idea queue

Stranger worlds still to build, in rough priority order. Each one runs by itself
and uses the grammar for its writing.

1. **The Lost Museum:** a night guard walks a museum of a civilisation that never existed, with generated exhibits and confident, wrong placards.
2. **Radio Static:** tune slowly across fictional stations: call-ins, shipping forecasts for invented seas, numbers stations and a continuing soap opera. Needs almost no art.
3. **Geology Mode:** one valley at extreme speed, with mountains, rivers, forests, ice ages and cities over millions of years.
4. **Tiny Bureaucracy:** a top-down office where paperwork moves under grammar-generated regulations, reusing The Game's rule engine.
5. **The Evolving Alphabet:** an imaginary language drifts and splits into dialects, with a growing dictionary and family tree.
6. **Weather Gods:** generated gods on tiny islands argue and send each other weather while villages change religion to suit.
7. **Parallel Mall:** an empty, softly lit 1980s mall generates itself as you drift through it.
8. **Slime Mould Civilisation:** a slime finds efficient routes across a map while tiny historians write its history as an empire.
9. **The Dream Elevator:** a generated floor directory, with a different little scene behind the doors each time.
10. **Letters Between Lighthouses:** two keepers trade Morse across a dark sea in a slow generated relationship.
