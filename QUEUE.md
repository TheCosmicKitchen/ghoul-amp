# WinVamp idea queue

Chill worlds still to build. Each one is a self-running little scene in the same
style as Pocket Village and Night Train, in rough priority order.

1. **Almanac Farm:** a pixel farm through four seasons, with crops, chickens and chores.
2. **The Long Sail:** a small boat crossing a calm sea, fishing and logging catches.
3. **Reef Tank:** a living aquarium with a species collection book and a tiny diver.
4. **Corner Café:** regulars, orders and speech bubbles, with rain outside.
5. **Lo-Fi Room:** someone reads, naps and changes records while plants grow and the cat moves.
6. **Long Trail:** a hiker and dog walk through forest, meadow and snow, and camp at night.
7. **Tiny Terrarium:** a small ecosystem of grass, rabbits, foxes and trees.
8. **Backyard Observatory:** a telescope, constellations, comets and meteor showers.
