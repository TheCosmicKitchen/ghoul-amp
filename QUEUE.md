# WinVamp idea queue

Chill worlds still to build. Each one is a self-running little scene in the same
style as the other cozy worlds, in rough priority order.

1. **Lo-Fi Room:** someone reads, naps and changes records while plants grow and the cat moves.
2. **Long Trail:** a hiker and dog walk through forest, meadow and snow, and camp at night.
3. **Tiny Terrarium:** a small ecosystem of grass, rabbits, foxes and trees.
4. **Backyard Observatory:** a telescope, constellations, comets and meteor showers.
